The numbers are computed on your machine. Gemini turns them into advice.

In the browser

  • Rasterise land cover to 784 cells
  • Surface, air and felt temperature
  • Getis-Ord Gi* hotspot detection
  • Budget optimiser and simulator

COOLTRACE server

  • Fetches and caches public data
  • Sends metrics to Gemini
  • Holds the key in .env

Open data

  • Open-Meteo forecast and ERA5 archive
  • OpenStreetMap via Overpass

Google Gemini

  • Hotspot explanations and briefings
  • Plan reviews and answers
The API key never reaches the browser, and no other paid service is needed.
